### 🚀 About Me

I'm an **AI/ML Engineer** pursuing an **M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Northeastern University (San Jose)**, with **14+ years of industry experience** building data platforms, ETL pipelines, and data-quality frameworks for **TransUnion, Barclays, American Express, Amex GBT, Capital One, and FedEx**.

My focus is where **production-scale data engineering meets applied ML in finance**: credit risk, fraud, financial NLP/QA, and derivatives market data. Years of running pipelines that process 1B+ records a day taught me how data flows, breaks, and scales in real systems, and I bring that discipline to model building: leakage-safe validation, reproducible experiments, and evaluation you can trust.

Most recently I was a **Data & AI Engineer (Co-op) at Evenness Inc.**, where I built a RAG workflow for regulatory compliance automation that cut manual analysis by 30%.

### 🔭 What I'm Working On Now

- 🧠 **Memorization in language models** — studying whether synthetic structured records leak faster than prose at controlled repetition counts, using LMs trained from scratch

- 📚 **Coursework:** Deep Learning (CS 7150) and Foundations of Generative AI (CS 6180): LLM pretraining, SFT/RL, diffusion, KV cache/vLLM, FlashAttention, quantization, agentic systems
